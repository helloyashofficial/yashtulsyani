Nobody has to shrink

A handbook for secure love, exploring neglect, self-worth, emotional independence, and the quantum philosophy of attention and possibility.

Neglect rarely announces itself. A question that is never answered, an achievement that receives no acknowledgment, an explanation treated as an excuse. One ignored conversation is nothing. A hundred of them become a belief.

It turns the mirror both ways: a chapter for the person being made small, and a harder one for the person doing it. The last third is a workbook rather than an argument.

The thing you were afraid of

A quantum philosophy of love, insecurity, validation and the reality we create.

Insecurity becomes reassurance-seeking. Reassurance becomes expectation. Expectation becomes pressure. Pressure changes behaviour. And then the person who feared a particular outcome points at it and says, I knew it.

Quantum mechanics enters as a philosophical vocabulary for possibility, unknowing and participation, not as a mechanism. The book is explicit about that line and returns to it repeatedly.
